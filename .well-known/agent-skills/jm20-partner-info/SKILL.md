# JM20 AI Solutions: how to get accurate information

JM20 AI Solutions (JM20) publishes its main website at https://jm20aisolutions.com. The site is in Spanish by default and has an English toggle.

## When to use this skill

- Someone asks what JM20 does, what problems it helps with, or what its approach is.
- Someone asks about the public demos Noah and Murph.
- Someone asks how to contact JM20.

## Where to find the facts

1. Read https://jm20aisolutions.com/llms.txt for a short summary of the company.
2. If your browser supports WebMCP, the main site registers these tools. Prefer them over guessing:
   - `get_business_info`: general information about JM20, its approach and its value proposition.
   - `get_services`: the six business problem areas JM20 helps with.
   - `get_case_studies`: the public demos of Noah and Murph, with links.
   - `calculate_business_losses`: estimates monthly and yearly losses using the four formulas of the JM20 calculator. Omitted fields use the example values from the website.
   - `contact_jm20`: fills in the contact form for the visitor to review.
3. Public demos are hosted at https://noah.jm20aisolutions.com and https://murph.jm20aisolutions.com.
4. AI-readiness checks and audits are described at https://aiready.jm20aisolutions.com.

## Rules for agents

- Do not invent prices, features, guarantees or availability. If the information is not on the website or returned by a tool, say so.
- `contact_jm20` only fills in the form. The visitor must review it, accept the consent box and send it manually. Never tell the user a message was sent.
- Answer in the language the user writes in.
